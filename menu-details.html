Pan-Seared King Oyster Scallops

$28.00
(4.95/5 from 210+ reviews)

Thick-cut hand-scored King Oyster mushroom medallions, slow-poached in kombu-infused herb broth and seared to golden caramelization. Served atop a velvety sweet garden pea and mint purée, roasted baby heirloom carrots, crispy shallot blossoms, and finished with a citrus Meyer lemon emulsion.

Chef's Special Add-Ons:
Ready for instant home delivery or dine-in ordering.

Farm-Sourced Ingredients

100% Organic
  • Organic King Oyster Mushrooms (Sonoma)
  • Sweet English Garden Peas (Willamette)
  • Fresh Meyer Lemon & Herb Emulsion
  • Heirloom Purple & Rainbow Baby Carrots
  • Crispy Golden Shallot Blossoms
  • Cold-Pressed Sicilian Olive Oil
Sonoma Valley Origin 18-Min Artisan Prep Served Sizzling Hot
