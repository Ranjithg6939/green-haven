Our Story

Born from Reverence for the Earth

Green Haven began with a vision: to elevate organic, biodynamic plant-based dining into a multisensory culinary celebration.

Founded by a collective of botanists, fermenters, and fine-dining chefs, we craft every dish using freshly harvested greenhouse herbs, small-batch cold-pressed dressings, and heirloom-certified grains.

The Green Haven Standard

Rooted in Purity, Crafted with Respect

Four foundational commitments that guide every harvest, plate, and pairing in our kitchen.

01

100% Biodynamic

Zero artificial pesticides, synthetic fertilizers, or GMO seeds. Only living soil ecology and heirloom heritage crops.

02

Regenerative Soil

Direct partnerships with 14 micro-farms using no-till practices and bio-composting to sequester carbon back into the earth.

03

Living Fermentation

House-aged nut cheeses, wild koji reductions, and probiotic vinegars cultivated slowly in our glasshouse fermentation lab.

04

Zero-Waste Kitchen

100% of organic scraps composted into greenhouse soil, solar-induction cooking hearths, and biodegradable packaging.

Cascade Valley Farms

Hood River, OR • Mt. Hood Valley Foothills
Sustainable Practice: 100% Closed-Loop Biodynamic Greenhouse Ecology

Cascade Valley Farms operates a zero-synthetic greenhouse bio-dome nestled at the base of Mt. Hood. Guided by Steiner biodynamic rhythms, they nurture living soils enriched with glacial volcanic mineral water, botanical compost teas, and celestial lunar cycles.

Partnership with Green Haven

Every morning at 5:30 AM, freshly harvested living micro-herbs, crimson borage blossoms, and petite heirloom greens are packed in reusable cedar crates and driven direct to Green Haven. Chef Elena uses their fiery radish cress and velvety pea tendrils to garnish our signature dishes and infuse raw elixirs.
